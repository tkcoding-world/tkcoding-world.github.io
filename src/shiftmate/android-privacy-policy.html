Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how ShiftMate ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our Android mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Storage

ShiftMate is designed to be privacy-first.

  • Local Storage: All your shift data, including schedules, hourly rates, and earnings calculations, is stored locally on your device in the App's private storage.
  • Android Backup: If you have enabled backup for your Google Account on your device, Android's Auto Backup service may back up the App's data to your own Google Drive, encrypted, and restore it when you reinstall the App or set up a new device. This backup is managed by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We (the developers) do not have access to your data, your schedule, or your earnings. We do not store your data on any external servers owned by us.

2. Permissions We Request

To provide key features, the App may request access to certain system permissions:

  • Calendar: We request calendar read and write permissions to sync your shifts to your device's system calendar. This is used solely to write shift events to your calendar. We do not read, scrape, or sell your personal calendar events.
  • Photos / Files: We may request access to save "Snapshots" (images of your schedule) to your device's photo gallery or files.

3. Analytics and Crash Data

We do not use third-party analytics tools (like Google Analytics or Mixpanel) to track your behavior.

Google may collect anonymous crash reports and usage statistics through Google Play (if you have opted in to share usage and diagnostics data in your Android settings). This data is anonymized and used solely to improve the App's stability.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

All payments and subscriptions are processed securely by Google via Google Play. We do not collect or store your credit card information or billing address.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services (e.g., email for support). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, providing notice within the App.
